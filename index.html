Meet Our Founder

A voice for the voiceless, a champion for change

Mandipa Amantle Hlabano

Driven by Purpose, Fueled by Compassion

The founder of Her Armour embarked on this journey after witnessing the devastating impact of gender-based violence in communities across Botswana. What began as a personal mission to support one survivor evolved into a comprehensive initiative to protect and empower women everywhere.

With a deep commitment to social justice and women's rights, our founder recognized that ending gender-based violence requires a multifaceted approach—combining education, practical self-defense tools, community support, and unwavering advocacy. Her Armour was born from this vision.

Through workshops, awareness campaigns, and the provision of safety resources, we've reached thousands of women, giving them the confidence, knowledge, and tools to protect themselves and break the cycle of violence. Every woman who walks away stronger is a victory for us all.

Every woman deserves to feel safe. Every survivor deserves support. Every voice deserves to be heard. Her Armour is not just an initiative—it's a movement to reclaim power, dignity, and freedom.

What We're Doing Now

Awareness Campaigns

Running community-wide campaigns to educate about consent, healthy relationships, and recognizing warning signs of abuse. Our campaigns reach schools, workplaces, and community centers across Botswana.

Self-Defense Training

Providing practical self-defense workshops to empower women with physical safety skills and confidence. Our training programs are designed for all skill levels and focus on real-world protection scenarios.

Support Networks

Building strong support systems and connecting survivors with counseling, legal aid, and community resources. We provide a safe space for healing, recovery, and empowerment.

Safety Resources

Distributing essential safety tools and resources through our Her Armour shop to help women protect themselves. Check out our products below for life-saving safety items.
