Our Mission & Values

The principles that guide everything we do

Our Mission

To empower women and end gender-based violence through education, advocacy, and practical support. We envision a Botswana where every woman lives free from fear, equipped with the knowledge, tools, and community support needed to protect herself and thrive. Through comprehensive programs that combine awareness campaigns, self-defense training, and survivor support, we are building a movement that transforms lives and creates lasting social change.

Our Values

  • Empowerment Equipping women with knowledge, skills, and confidence to protect themselves and claim their rights.
  • Compassion Providing judgment-free support and understanding to all survivors of violence.
  • Community Building strong networks of support that create lasting change together.
  • Action Moving beyond awareness to tangible solutions that save lives.

The Founder's Journey

Her Armour was founded with a singular vision: to create a world where every woman feels safe, empowered, and protected. The founder's journey began after witnessing the devastating impact of gender-based violence in communities across Botswana, where too many women suffered in silence, lacking the resources and support to break free.

What started as a grassroots effort to support one survivor evolved into a comprehensive initiative that combines education, practical tools, and community advocacy. With a background in social work and a deep commitment to women's rights, the founder recognized that ending gender-based violence requires more than awareness—it demands action, resources, and systematic change.

Today, Her Armour stands as a beacon of hope and strength, providing thousands of women with self-defense training, safety resources, counseling support, and a powerful community that refuses to stay silent. Every workshop taught, every safety device distributed, and every survivor supported represents a step toward our ultimate goal: a Botswana free from gender-based violence.

Every woman deserves to feel safe. Every survivor deserves support. Every voice deserves to be heard. Her Armour is not just an initiative—it's a movement to reclaim power, dignity, and freedom for all women.

What We're Doing Now

Awareness Campaigns

Running community-wide campaigns to educate about consent, healthy relationships, and recognizing warning signs of abuse. Our campaigns reach schools, workplaces, and community centers across Botswana.

Self-Defense Training

Providing practical self-defense workshops to empower women with physical safety skills and confidence. Our training programs are designed for all skill levels and focus on real-world protection scenarios.

Support Networks

Building strong support systems and connecting survivors with counseling, legal aid, and community resources. We provide a safe space for healing, recovery, and empowerment.

Safety Resources

Distributing essential safety tools and resources through our Her Armour shop to help women protect themselves. Check out our products below for life-saving safety items.
