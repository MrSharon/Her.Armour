Meet Our Founder

A voice for the voiceless, a champion for change

Mandipa Amantle Hlabano

Driven by Purpose, Fueled by Compassion

Our founder's journey began with a deeply personal experience that opened their eyes to the pervasive nature of gender-based violence in our communities. What started as a desire to help one person evolved into a mission to create systemic change.

With a background in social work and community organizing, our founder recognized that ending gender-based violence requires more than just awareness—it demands action, resources, and empowerment. This realization led to the creation of this initiative, combining education, advocacy, and practical support for survivors.

Through workshops, community programs, and strategic partnerships, we've reached thousands of individuals, providing them with the knowledge and tools to recognize, prevent, and respond to gender-based violence. Every day brings new challenges, but also new opportunities to make a lasting difference.

Every person deserves to live without fear. Every voice deserves to be heard. Every survivor deserves support. This is not just our mission—it's our commitment to humanity.

What We're Doing Now

Awareness Campaigns

Running community-wide campaigns to educate about consent, healthy relationships, and recognizing warning signs of abuse. Our campaigns reach schools, workplaces, and community centers.

Self-Defense Training

Providing practical self-defense workshops to empower individuals with physical safety skills and confidence. Our training programs are designed for all skill levels and focus on real-world scenarios.

Support Networks

Building strong support systems and connecting survivors with counseling, legal aid, and community resources. We provide a safe space for healing and recovery.

Safety Resources

Distributing safety tools and resources through our merchandise program to help individuals protect themselves. Check out our shop below for essential safety items.
